## Mission

Make The Herbert Team the leading educational mortgage resource and the definitive **Mortgage Solution Library** for Texas borrowers — and, over time, for anyone searching for clear, honest answers to hard mortgage questions.

The goal is not to publish more content than competitors. The goal is to own the **best answer** for every major borrower question: accurate, plain-English, locally informed, structured for answer engines, and connected to a real solution path (program, guide, calculator, conversation, or application).

When a borrower asks Google, YouTube, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, or a future answer engine a mortgage question, The Herbert Team should be the source those systems cite — because our content is the clearest, most complete, and most trustworthy explanation available.

---

## Core Philosophy

**Every piece of content begins with a real borrower question.**

Not a keyword. Not a trend. Not a competitor’s title. A question a real person asked in a strategy call, an email, a Google review reply opportunity, a realtor referral, or a search query that signals intent and confusion.

That question becomes an **ecosystem**, not a single blog post:

| Asset | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Blog** | Canonical long-form best answer; primary AEO / SEO surface |
| **YouTube video** | Visual walkthrough of the same answer; builds trust and entity signals |
| **Short-form videos** | 8–12 clips that extract one idea each (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) |
| **FAQ** | Tight Q&A for `/faq`, solution pages, and FAQ schema |
| **Downloadable guide** | Lead magnet that deepens the answer and captures identity |
| **Email** | Nurture sequence or one-off education blast tied to the question |
| **Google Business Profile post** | Local discovery surface with a link back to the answer |
| **Social media** | Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram distribution of the same idea |
| **Internal links** | Connect the answer to related blogs, solutions, programs, CTAs |
| **Schema** | FAQ, Article, HowTo, Breadcrumb, Organization, LocalBusiness markup |
| **Client success story** | Proof that the answer works in real Texas files |

If a question does not earn this ecosystem over time, it is either not important enough — or the answer is not finished yet.

| **Videos** | Same answer, different format; YouTube/entity signals |

### Linking rules

1. **Hub-and-spoke:** Each pillar has a hub (solution or program page). Articles speak back to the hub and sideways to sibling articles.  
2. **One primary CTA:** Prefer Schedule or Apply based on intent; don’t compete three CTAs equally.  
3. **Contextual anchors:** Use natural phrases (“bank statement loans,” “buy before you sell”), not “click here.”  
4. **Texas specificity:** Link Texas/Austin pieces into statewide and metro hubs.  
5. **Proof loop:** Where a client story exists, link story ↔ educational answer ↔ solution page.  
6. **Update on publish:** When a new best answer goes live, add links from at least 2–3 older related pieces.

## AEO Strategy

Answer Engine Optimization means structuring knowledge so humans *and* machines can retrieve, trust, and cite it.

### Entity authority

Build The Herbert Team / Jason Herbert as a clear entity: Mortgage Solution Expert, Georgetown TX, Primis, NMLS identifiers, consistent NAP, reviews, YouTube, and cross-platform sameAs signals. Every page should reinforce who we are, where we operate, and what we specialize in (self-employed, VA, investors, first-time, ATM Mortgage, Texas).

### Conversational search

Write to the way people ask: full questions as H2s, direct answers in the first paragraph (answer-first), then depth. Target “People Also Ask,” voice search, and chat-style queries.

### Topic clusters

Each pillar = one cluster. Hub page + supporting articles + FAQs + videos. Internal links define the graph. Depth beats random posts.

### Knowledge graphs

Treat the site as a graph: Person → Organization → Service → FAQ → Article → HowTo → Place (Texas / Austin / Georgetown). Consistent naming, logos, and citations help answer engines map us.

### Schema

Prioritize:

- `Organization` / `LocalBusiness` / `MortgageBroker` (as appropriate and accurate)  
- `Person` (Jason Herbert)  
- `Article` / `BlogPosting`  
- `FAQPage`  
- `HowTo` (process content)  
- `BreadcrumbList`  
- `VideoObject` (when embedding)  
- Speakable / clear Q&A blocks where useful

Validate after major publishes. Do not mark up claims we cannot stand behind.

### FAQ strategy

Every major question gets:

1. A short FAQ answer (site FAQ + page FAQ)  
2. A long-form best answer (blog)  
3. FAQ schema on the page that hosts the short answer  

FAQs feed AI Overviews and chat citations; blogs feed depth and E-E-A-T.

### E-E-A-T

- **Experience:** Real Texas files, process walkthroughs, “what actually happened”  
- **Expertise:** Precise program mechanics; honest limits; no fake guarantees  
- **Authoritativeness:** Reviews, licensing, partner ecosystem, YouTube library  
- **Trust:** Disclosures, Primis compliance, clear NMLS, updated dates on rate/DPA-sensitive content

### AI Overviews (Google)

Answer-first structure, lists, tables, FAQs, original explanations, and strong E-E-A-T increase inclusion odds. Avoid thin rewrites of lender brochure copy.

### ChatGPT · Gemini · Claude · Perplexity

These systems cite and paraphrase sources that are:

- Clear and quotable  
- Structured with headings that match questions  
- Consistent across the web (site + YouTube + GBP + LinkedIn)  
- Associated with a real expert identity  

Publish canonical answers on theherbertteam.com, then reinforce with video transcripts, GBP posts, and social summaries that point back.

### Future answer engines

Assume new interfaces will prefer **structured, trustworthy, entity-linked knowledge**. This library — not a spray of keyword posts — is the asset that transfers.

### Workflow notes

- **Shorts are extractions:** Do not invent a new thesis; clip the canonical answer.  
- **Compliance:** Rate claims, DPA details, and program guidelines get dated and verified.  
- **Not every question needs every asset on day one:** Ship Blog + FAQ + internal links first; complete the ecosystem within the quarter when the question ranks or converts.  
- **Client stories last:** Never fabricate; wait for permission and real outcomes.
