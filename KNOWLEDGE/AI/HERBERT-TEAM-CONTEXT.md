# AEO and Content Strategy

- Every content asset should answer one primary borrower question
- Prioritize direct, useful answers
- Use conversational headings such as:
  - Can I…
  - Should I…
  - What happens if…
  - How does…
- Build topic clusters and strong internal links
- Include FAQs where useful
- Support claims with real experience and clearly stated qualifications
- Never keyword-stuff
